## 2. Business Need
The project draft describes manual campus pickup requests, difficulty coordinating collection schedules, limited recycling incentives and the absence of centralized collection records. The proposed system addresses these needs by organizing requests and collection records and supporting recycling participation. Validate this description of the current process through interviews or a questionnaire before treating it as an observed finding.

## 3. Business Requirements
- Students and staff submit pickup requests with location, material type and estimated quantity, and track request status.
- An administrator schedules pickups and assigns collection workers.
- Workers record actual accepted recyclable weight and confirm completed collection.
- The system awards points once for each verified collection; users redeem available rewards subject to their balance and reward availability.
- Administrators view reports on requests, collected materials and recycling participation.
- Role-based access separates user, worker and administrator capabilities.

These are high-level business capabilities. Detailed functional requirements, measurable quality requirements and precise point/reward rules belong in the later requirements specification.

## 4. Business Value
Expected benefits include less time spent coordinating requests, improved tracking of pickups, fewer missed or delayed collections, increased recycling participation and more reliable information for campus decisions. These are expected benefits, not measured results. Assess them using coordination time, pickup completion/delay, verified recyclable weight, participation and reward cost. Establish baselines and targets from actual findings and the feasibility analysis; no numerical improvement is claimed here.

## 5. Special Issues and Constraints
- Limit the initial proposal to one campus and the core collection/request/reward workflow.
- Final documentation deadline in the supplied course agenda: 30 December 2026.
- Award points only once per verified collection; use accepted actual weight rather than the user's estimate.
- Define eligible materials, point conversion and redemption rules before implementation.
- Limit rewards to the approved budget and availability.
- Protect personal data and restrict access by role.
- Obtain campus authorization and access to collection staff and weighing equipment before an operational pilot.
- Smart sensors, route optimization and external integrations are outside the initial scope unless separately approved.
